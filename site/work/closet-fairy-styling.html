The briefCloset Fairy Styling

Sara Mahdavi was our very first client. She came to us as she was starting Closet Fairy and stepping into a new era as a personal stylist. She needed a brand that sounded like her, a presence that showed how she works, and an audience that would become clients.

Chapter IMay 2023

Finding the voice

We began at the beginning: who Closet Fairy is, how she speaks and how she shows up. We defined the brand voice, and the way Sara would appear on social media as she stepped into her new era.

From there we took over her social media entirely, and carried the same voice onto her website through copywriting and blog posts.

DeliveredBrand voiceSocial media strategyCopywritingBlog posts
Chapter IIThe signature series

Client Journeys

We created Client Journeys, a series that follows Sara's signature styling process from the first conversation to the finished wardrobe, with each client's result as the reveal.

It remains her best-performing content. A single video reached 538,000 views and brought in more than $145,000 of inbound enquiries. We ran the series for every client thereafter.

Chapter IIIThrough October 2025

Everything else, handled

Around Client Journeys we built the rest of the content mix, and every format earned its place among her top performers. We wrote, scripted, directed and edited all of it, and managed the account day to day.

We also turned Client Journeys into advertising, with A/B versions for paid campaigns, and directed three content shoots, including one in New York.
