The briefPower Dental Group

Power Dental Group had spent twenty years and twenty-five US patents earning the respect of the profession. Online, the company was rather quieter than its reputation. When Kyle Lim took the helm from his father, he wanted the brand to look, sound and perform like the market leader it already was.

Chapter IThe foundations
I

Finding the voice

Dental, as a category, tends to look sterile and clinical. We wanted Power Dental Group to feel nothing like the surgery, so we looked beyond medicine entirely, to luxury manufacturing: fine watchmaking, performance cars and aviation.

It was not an obvious starting point, which is precisely why it worked. These are the worlds many dentists admire in their own time. A look and feel built around their passions, rather than around selling them more work, gave a company of precision engineers a presence to match its craft.

From there we defined the messaging pillars and tone of voice, and refined how the brand looks, sounds and conducts itself online.

Chapter IIQ4 2024
II

Finding the rhythm

With the identity settled, we turned to the feed. Together with our production partner we built a social strategy suited to the business, settled the team into a weekly filming rhythm, and media-trained them on how they speak, present and carry themselves on camera.

The audience noticed. In nine months the Instagram following grew from 400 to 4,500, and the market began to treat Power Dental Group as one of its leading voices.

Chapter IIIQ2 2025
III

Finding the audience

Then we raised the stakes. In Q2 2025 we ideated, introduced and directed three new content series. Each ran for around three months, each went viral, and the team still films all three today.

Alongside them we directed a run of mid-form vlogs. By the close of 2025 Instagram had reached 48,400 followers, with TikTok up 10,000 and YouTube up 5,000. No dental instrument manufacturer performed better online.
