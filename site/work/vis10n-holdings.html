The briefVIS10N Holdings

VIS10N Holdings invests in and builds ventures across sport, media, marketing, technology and real estate. Its founder, RayJ Dennis, also happens to play in the NBA. The company had to carry itself like an institution from the day it opened its doors, without borrowing hours its CEO simply does not have.

Chapter IThe foundations

Defining the identity

We placed VIS10N where sport becomes enterprise: a partner-led holding company with an athlete's feel for the game and an investor's patience. The voice, the identity and the manner in which VIS10N introduces itself to founders, athletes and brands all take their cue from there.

The website has had two editions. The first opened the doors. The second furnished the house, with VIS10N's convictions, companies, founder and point of view all under one roof.

Chapter IIIOperations

Running the business

A holding company cannot wait for its CEO to finish practice. We built the structure VIS10N runs on and took the day-to-day off his hands: systems, processes and client management, quietly kept in order.

The result is a business that behaves impeccably whether or not its founder is in the room.
